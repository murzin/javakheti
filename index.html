BEHIND THE JOURNEY

A place to look closer.

Javakheti is a highland region in southern Georgia. Its national park and surrounding managed reserves protect a landscape of lakes, grasslands and volcanic mountains.

Land & water

Great Abuli rises to 3,300 metres. Lake Paravani is Georgia’s largest lake. The region’s cold continental winters can keep its lakes frozen for long periods.

A protected landscape

Established in 2011, the protected areas include Javakheti National Park and the Kartsakhi, Sulda, Khanchali, Bugdasheni and Madatapa managed reserves. The lakes support nesting and migratory birds.
